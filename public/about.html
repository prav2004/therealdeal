On Sports, you choose games and make practice picks using virtual tokens. Odds are shown per selection, and your slip calculates combined odds automatically. No real money is wagered.
Pickr is 100% free-to-play. No deposits are required. Pickr Tokens are virtual practice credits with no cash value. They cannot be purchased, withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes.
Tasks include daily and weekly quests, streak tracking, and the daily spin. Claim rewards after completing each goal to stack tokens and XP.
The leaderboard ranks players by combined points (wins, streaks, and XP momentum). Keep climbing with consistent performance.
Picks shows your recent predictions, odds, token amounts used, and performance snapshots across week, month, and year views.
Profile shows your account details and virtual token balance. Tokens have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn — Pickr does not process deposits, withdrawals, or cash payouts.
