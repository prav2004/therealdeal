How Pickr Works

Pickr is a free-to-play sports prediction platform where you practise making picks using virtual tokens. No deposits are ever required and no real money is wagered.

Pickr Tokens are virtual practice credits with no cash value. They cannot be purchased, withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes.

How a practice pick works:

Choose an outcome, decide how many virtual tokens to use, and submit your pick. Pickr shows a potential virtual-token return so you can track how your prediction would have performed.

Your result is recorded in your Pickr history so you can build your sports knowledge and track your performance over time.

Optional partner offers:

When you feel ready, you can explore optional offers from licensed betting partners. Partner websites are separate from Pickr, and their eligibility requirements, terms, and responsible-gambling rules apply.
